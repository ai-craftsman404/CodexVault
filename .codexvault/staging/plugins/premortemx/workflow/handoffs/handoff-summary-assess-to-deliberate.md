## Skim Summary

This handoff defines the minimum governed payload that moves an assessed case into deliberation without making the next stage rediscover the basics.

## From And To

- Asset Type: `Handoff Summary`
- Purpose: define the standard assess-to-deliberate handoff contract
- From Stage: `Assess`
- To Stage: `Deliberate`

## Decision Made

- evidence bundle is sufficient to form an initial risk picture
- at least one risk theme or one material evidence gap has been recorded

## Decision Rationale

- the next stage should deliberate on structured findings, not raw notes
- evidence weakness must stay visible so the next stage does not overstate certainty

## Evidence Used

- inspected evidence bundle references
- evidence gap note if present
- bounded scope from intake

## Open Issues

- unresolved evidence conflicts
- missing controls or ownership data
- any ambiguity that could affect approval posture

## Next Owner

- `Orchestrator and specialist deliberation roles`

## Next Required Action

- compare specialist risk views
- weigh evidence strength and completeness
- prepare adjudicated recommendation or escalation
